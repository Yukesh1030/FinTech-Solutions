About STACKLY FinTech

Building Better
Financial Experiences.

We believe financial technology should be invisible, instantaneous, and radically transparent. Here is why we build and who we are.

How We Started

Born to Eliminate Commercial Financial Friction

STACKLY was founded by financial engineers and distributed systems architects who experienced first-hand how legacy banking bottlenecks stifle modern business growth.

From days-long settlement cycles to opaque transaction rejections and manual invoice reconciliation, financial teams were drowning in administrative overhead. We set out to build a unified financial operating system that moves money at internet speed.

Read Founder Manifesto

Our Mission

To empower enterprises and builders with transparent, high-throughput financial rails that turn complex commercial treasury workflows into simple, real-time code.

  • Zero hidden spreads or arbitrary holding periods.
  • 100% API availability and predictable SLAs.

Our Vision

A frictionless global economy where any business can instantly dispatch, receive, and account for value across any currency, rail, or platform with complete cryptographic certainty.

  • Universal cross-border liquidity without delays.
  • Continuous automated regulatory compliance.
Engineering Philosophy

Our Approach to Financial Technology

Three core pillars guide our architecture, ensuring reliability and institutional trust.

Zero-Latency Architecture

Every transaction is processed through ultra-low latency memory buses and event streams, achieving sub-second authorisations worldwide.

Extreme Defense-in-Depth

We assume breach conditions at every tier. Data is fragmented, encrypted at rest with hardware HSMs, and access is audited continuously.

Human-First Clarity

Financial software shouldn't require a finance PhD. We design clean, calm interfaces that deliver instant comprehension without cognitive clutter.

Design Standards

Product Design Principles

Trust Commitments

Verifiable Data Sovereignty & Protection

We hold ourselves to rigorous compliance and ethical standards. We never sell customer transaction data, monetize order flow, or compromise client confidentiality.

  • AES-256 military-grade encryption for all database records.
  • Continuous penetration testing by independent CREST auditors.
  • Transparent incident reporting with public uptime dashboards.
